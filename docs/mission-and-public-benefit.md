# Mission and Public Benefit

LocalAIServers is a 501(c)(3) public charity providing public education and open-source
infrastructure for locally hosted AI systems.

## Public Education Mission

LocalAIServers publishes practical documentation, deployment scripts, benchmark methods,
QC standards, and reproducibility workflows for locally hosted AI systems. The goal is
to make infrastructure knowledge inspectable and reusable for people who do not have
access to institutional clusters, enterprise cloud budgets, or current-generation
accelerators.

## Why Affordable Local AI Infrastructure Matters

AI infrastructure education is often gated by expensive hardware, closed platforms, or
operational knowledge that is not written down. LocalAIServers focuses on reproducible
local systems so students, educators, independent researchers, nonprofit technologists,
community AI builders, and under-resourced learners can understand how these systems are
built, measured, and maintained.

## Why GFX906 Preservation Matters

GFX906 / MI50-class hardware remains a useful public-interest learning target because it
is capable enough for meaningful local inference work but difficult to operate without
careful runtime, kernel, and verification notes. Preserving working paths for this
hardware class helps communities learn from affordable AI research infrastructure
instead of depending only on newer accelerators.

## Controlled Infrastructure, Public Outputs

LocalAIServers uses controlled compute infrastructure to validate hardware, reproduce
workloads, test runtime changes, and produce public artifacts. Public benefit is
delivered through code, documentation, benchmark reports, QC methods, source-level
findings, and reproducible workflows rather than direct machine access.

## Public-Benefit Boundaries

LocalAIServers does not operate as a hardware reseller, public cloud provider, financial
service, medical service, or insurance service. The repository is for public education
and open-source infrastructure documentation.

## Beneficiaries

The intended beneficiaries are students, educators, independent researchers, nonprofit
technologists, community AI builders, and under-resourced learners who need practical AI
infrastructure knowledge but lack access to institutional clusters, enterprise cloud
budgets, or current-generation accelerators.
