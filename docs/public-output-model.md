# Public Output Model

LocalAIServers does not need to provide interactive cluster access to create public benefit.
The public benefit comes from reproducible methods, published results, source-level
findings, and verification standards.

## Why The Cluster Is Controlled

Controlled infrastructure lets LocalAIServers preserve reproducibility, hardware
custody, known software images, and repeatable validation conditions. That matters when
the goal is to publish trustworthy methods and benchmark evidence rather than operate a
hosted compute service.

## What Outputs Are Public

- Deployment scripts and runtime configuration.
- Docker image identities, archive hashes, and reproducibility notes.
- Benchmark summaries and methodology.
- QC methods and hardware verification standards.
- Source-level findings and rejected-path notes.
- Educational documentation.

## How Outputs Are Generated

Outputs are generated through controlled hardware verification, reproducible runtime
setup, benchmark runs, source/runtime experiments, and redacted public documentation.
Private operational details are not required for the public to benefit from the methods
and findings.

## How Outputs Support Education

The repository gives readers concrete examples of how locally hosted AI systems are
configured, tested, measured, and documented. It also shows how negative results and
rejected paths reduce duplicated work.

## How Outputs Reduce Fraud And Failure Risk

QC methods, measured VRAM checks, runtime validation, and reproducible benchmark reports
help the public evaluate used AI hardware and avoid preventable setup failures.

## How Outputs Support Affordable AI Research Access

By preserving working paths on GFX906 / MI50-class hardware and publishing what does and
does not work, LocalAIServers lowers the documentation burden for community-scale
research infrastructure.
