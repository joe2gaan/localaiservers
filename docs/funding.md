# Funding

LocalAIServers is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity focused on public education around locally hosted AI servers.

## Safe Donation Language

Suggested donation call to action:

> Support open-source maintenance, reproducible local AI deployment notes, QC methodology, benchmark reporting, and community AI research access through LocalAIServers.

Do not replace this placeholder in the public repository until the donation page has been reviewed for public release.

## What Donations Support

Donations support:

- Public documentation for locally hosted AI servers.
- Open-source maintenance of deployment notes, issue templates, and benchmark reporting formats.
- QC methodology and hardware verification standards.
- Reproducible local AI deployment notes for GFX906 / MI50-class hardware.
- Community AI research access and affordable AI research access.
- Public education material for students, educators, independent researchers, and community builders.

## What Donations Do Not Provide

Donations do not provide:

- Hardware.
- Priority access.
- Discounts.
- Program participation.
- Procurement access.
- Shipping, fulfillment, or pass-through cost coverage.
- Any private benefit in exchange for the donation.

## No Goods or Services Statement

Charitable donations are intended to support public-benefit work. Unless specifically disclosed by LocalAIServers in a reviewed public donation receipt or campaign page, no goods or services are provided in exchange for donations.

## Payment Boundary

This repository must not include Wise links, personal payment links, routing details, account details, or hardware program payment links. Program payments for hardware verification, pass-through costs, or fulfillment are separate from charitable donations and should not be represented as charitable gifts.

## Suggested CTA Variants

- Donate to support public documentation, open-source maintenance, QC methodology, benchmark reporting, and community AI research access.
- Help maintain reproducible local AI deployment notes and hardware verification standards for community AI infrastructure.
- Support affordable AI research access through public education and open-source infrastructure documentation.
