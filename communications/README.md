# Communications Templates

These templates are generic starting points for public updates. Review dates, links, and
claims before posting. Do not add private operations data, participant data, payment
details, vendor details, private hostnames, private IPs, or unredacted logs.

## Templates

- [Donation announcement](donation-announcement-template.md)
- [GitHub project update](github-project-update-template.md)
- [Subreddit technical update](subreddit-technical-update-template.md)

## Public Wording Rules

- Keep claims grounded in committed repository evidence.
- Label future work as planned or future direction.
- Keep donations separate from hardware verification, pass-through costs, and
  fulfillment.
- Do not imply donors receive hardware, priority access, discounts, program
  participation, or private benefits.
- Do not claim official ROCm support or AMD endorsement.
