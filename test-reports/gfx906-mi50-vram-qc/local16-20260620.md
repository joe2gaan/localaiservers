## Results

- 1 GiB single-device smoke: PASS
- 30 GiB single-device MI50-32GB field check: expected FAIL
- Failure reason: `hipMalloc(32212254720 bytes)` returned `hipErrorOutOfMemory`

The result confirms that the maintained tool can distinguish a HIP-visible GFX906-class
card that can pass a small memory check from a card that cannot satisfy the MI50 32GB
large-VRAM field check.

## Interpretation

The maintained LocalAIServers MI50 / GFX906 VRAM QC tool behaved correctly on a local
16GB GFX906-class card. Passing the 1 GiB smoke indicates that HIP could see the device
and complete a transient allocation/check. Failing the 30 GiB run indicates that the
device did not satisfy the MI50 32GB large-VRAM gate. This is educational diagnostic
evidence only. It is not a certification, warranty, formal assurance program, support contract, official AMD
validation, buying/selling workflow, or a guarantee of AI workload performance.
