## Interpretation

The maintained LocalAIServers MI50 / GFX906 VRAM QC tool was validated on the `.20`
GFX906 server as an educational hardware-verification field check. Passing this tool
indicates that the selected devices were visible to ROCm/HIP and could allocate and
verify the requested VRAM region during this test. It is not certification, warranty,
official AMD validation, procurement support, resale support, or a guarantee of AI
workload performance.
